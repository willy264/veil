How it works

Veil uses a NEP-413 signed message to derive your keys in this browser. A sender creates a fresh implicit account for each payment. Your viewing key and a view tag help your inbox find the payments that belong to you.

Amounts, timing, senders and token contracts remain public. Sending funds back to your named account creates a public link.

Veil is a testnet prototype. Amounts, timing and senders remain public. Keep the same wallet signing key to recover your private inbox.
