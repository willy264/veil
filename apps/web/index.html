How it works

Veil uses a NEP-413 signed message to derive spending and viewing keys in your browser. Registration publishes only their public keys.

A sender derives a fresh implicit account for each payment and posts an announcement. Your viewing key and a view tag help your inbox find the matching payments.

The auditor key is your private viewing key: a 32-byte value written as 64 hexadecimal characters. It reveals incoming payments, but cannot sign a spend.

Amounts, timing, senders and token contracts stay public. Combining balances or sending to a named account can reveal links. Send payments sequentially; concurrent sends can affect discovery in this prototype.

Keep the same wallet signing key. Registrations made with older random signatures may need their original key setup.

Veil is a testnet prototype. Amounts, timing and senders remain public.
